How it works

Pitch once. Share only if you opt in.

Open Investor Pitch Format, OIPF, is a shared way to submit a pitch. Founders choose whether members can see it. Members keep their own intake and can exchange opted-in pitches.

  1. 1

    Pitch once

    Send a deck, or answer a set of structured questions. Do it here, or on any OIPF-compatible application run by a VC, accelerator, or partner.

  2. 2

    Opt in to share

    You choose whether the pitch goes into the Open Investor Pitch database. If you don’t opt in, it is not shared.

  3. 3

    Members discover and reach out

    Member VCs, accelerators, and partners can see opted-in pitches and may contact you to invest. One pitch reaches many investors.

Founders

One pitch. You choose if it is shared.

Submit as little as your pitch deck, or as much as a set of structured questions. Do it on this site, or on any OIPF-compatible application a VC, accelerator, or partner runs.

Opt in to the Open Investor Pitch database, and every member VC, accelerator, and partner can see your pitch and may contact you to invest. You decide whether it is shared. If you don’t opt in, members do not get it from the central database.

Investors

Keep your own intake.

VCs and accelerators keep the forms they already use: Airtable, Typeform, a custom application, or anything else. The workflow does not have to move.

If that form and workflow are OIPF-compatible, deals from founders who opt in to sharing flow into the central database. Members can also pull pitches from that database, whether they came in through the form here or through any member’s OIPF-compatible form.

Partners

Send pitches in. Pull them out.

Partners work the same way as investors. Keep your own inbound format. If it is OIPF-compatible, opted-in pitches come into the central database, and you can pull pitches out.
