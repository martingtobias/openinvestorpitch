Find the right investor without pitching fifty times

Fundraising is an opaque market. Founders tell the same story over and over. Investors see companies that were never for them, and have no clean way to send them on. Open Investor Pitch is a free way to pitch once, and for the right people to find each other.

Why it matters

Vision

Less friction between a founder and the fund that would actually say yes.

Most founders never meet the investor who wanted their company. Most investors never see the raise they were looking for. What sits in between is warm intros, forwarded decks, and a different format every time.

Open Investor Pitch is a shared way to pitch, so a company can be read, passed on, or picked up without starting over. It is free. There is no account, and no product to log into yet. Join the list to follow it, or to say you want to take part.
